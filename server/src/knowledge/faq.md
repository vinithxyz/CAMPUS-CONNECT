# CampusConnect Knowledge Base & FAQs

## Campus Issue Reporting
CampusConnect is the central portal for students, staff, and faculty to report, track, and resolve facility and infrastructure issues across campus.

## Categories
- Electrical: Power outages, faulty wiring, malfunctioning outlets, switches, fans, lights, or equipment.
- Plumbing and Water: Leaks, pipe bursts, blocked drains, low water pressure, broken taps, or restroom fixture failures.
- Mechanical: Elevator breakdowns, HVAC/air conditioning failures, noisy machinery, ventilation issues.
- Civil and Infrastructure: Structural cracks, broken tiles/pavement, damaged doors, windows, locks, furniture, roof leakage.
- Sanitation: Trash accumulation, pest control, unhygienic restrooms, common area cleanliness.
- Wi-Fi and Internet: Network connectivity drops, weak Wi-Fi signal, access point malfunctions in hostels and academic blocks.
- Hostel: Room fixtures, hostel amenities, geysers, drinking water coolers, common room facilities.
- Canteen: Mess hygiene, food service facilities, seating, dining area equipment.
- Safety: Fire extinguisher issues, emergency exit obstructions, exposed wires, slip hazards, broken railings, security lighting.
- Other: Any campus maintenance issue not covered above.

## Complaint Statuses
- Pending: The complaint has been received by CampusConnect and is awaiting review/assignment.
- In Progress: A maintenance technician or campus team has been assigned and is actively working on the issue.
- Resolved: The issue has been fixed and confirmed by campus facilities.

## Priority Levels
- Low: Cosmetic or minor issues with minimal operational impact (e.g. loose door handle, non-critical light fixture).
- Medium: Standard maintenance requests that do not pose immediate safety risks (e.g. leaking tap, single fan not working).
- High: Significant issues affecting daily academic or hostel living (e.g. entire room power outage, Wi-Fi outage before exams).
- Critical: Urgent safety hazards or severe disruptions (e.g. active flooding, electrical sparks, structural damage). Priority is chosen by the student when reporting; use Critical strictly for danger to people or property.

## Photos and Attachments
- Photos are optional: up to 5 photos per complaint (JPG or PNG, max 2 MB each). Photos help technicians diagnose issues faster.

## Campus Heat Map
- The interactive Campus Map shows an overlay heat layer of open issues across campus zones.
- Areas with higher complaint density appear red/warmer; areas with fewer issues appear green/cooler.

## Expected Resolution Times
- Critical: Within 2 to 4 hours.
- High: Within 12 to 24 hours.
- Medium: Within 24 to 48 hours.
- Low: Within 3 to 5 business days.

## Reporting Procedure
1. Navigate to "Report Issue" in the sidebar.
2. Select the issue category, enter a descriptive title, provide details, select your building and room number.
3. Pin the exact location on the campus map.
4. Review and submit. Track progress anytime in "My Complaints".

## Emergency Notice
For immediate danger, active fire, electrical sparks, medical emergencies, or security threats, call Campus Security at +1 (555) 019-2834 or visit the Security Control Room immediately. Do not rely on the chatbot or wait for a ticket response.
